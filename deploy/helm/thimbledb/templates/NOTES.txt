Check readiness:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ include "thimbledb.fullname" . }} 8787:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl http://127.0.0.1:8787/readyz

The local provider is for one-pod development only. Production replicas must
share S3, R2, or Azure Blob storage.
